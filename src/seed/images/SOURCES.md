# Where our pictures come from

Every picture here is a drawing our team made: a coloured plate with music staff lines and one large
icon from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev), drawn at 1200 by 800 px (a JPEG, quality 82). None shows a person
or a real place.

The icons are shared under the [ISC licence](https://lucide.dev/license), which allows using and changing
them as long as their copyright and licence notice goes with them. The site's about page says the same,
under "Picture credits" (the seed writes it: `PICTURE_CREDITS` in `src/seed/seed.ts`).

When you add a picture, use one you are allowed to use (your own, or one with an open licence) and record
it here the same way. Never upload photos of people to this site.
