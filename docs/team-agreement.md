# Team agreement

How our team works together. Every member reads it at the start, and we read it again when something goes wrong.

## Members, roles and pairs

We are a test team of two, so each of us holds three roles and we work as one pair.

| Member | Roles |
| --- | --- |
| Test A | Releases and repository; content model and data; the transactional task (booking a room) |
| Test B | The informational task (finding the right room); design and accessibility; research and testing |

We pair on the content model, because both tasks depend on it.

## How we communicate

We talk in our team chat and answer within one working day. We meet once a week after class for 30 minutes.
Every decision goes into the chat summary of that meeting, and a bigger product or technical choice gets a decision
record in docs/decisions/.

## How we decide

We talk it through and try the simplest option first. When we still disagree, the member who holds the role for that
part decides, and writes down why.

## When a pull request is done

A pull request is merged only when the starter's checks are green, the other member has reviewed and approved it, the
preview works for the change, and the AI note is filled in. The reviewer opens the preview and reads Files changed line
by line.

## The AI note

Every pull request says what we asked an AI tool and what we checked ourselves, or "No AI used." We never merge code
an AI tool wrote before we have run it and read it.

## When someone stops contributing

If a member misses two agreed tasks in a row, the other member talks to them within two days. If nothing changes in
the next week, we write down what happened and share the work again in this file.
